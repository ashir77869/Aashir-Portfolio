Onboarding

How we'll work together

A simple five-step process from your first message to a finished video.

1

Brief & Upload

Client sends over raw footage, references, and any instructions through whichever platform's easiest for them.

2

Review & Confirm

I go through everything to make sure I have what's needed, then confirm the direction, style, and timeline before starting.

3

Editing

I edit based on the approved brief and selected package — pacing, sound design, color, and motion all handled here.

4

Revisions

Client reviews the draft and sends feedback. I apply changes until it feels right.

5

Final Delivery

Finished video is delivered in the agreed format, ready to post.
